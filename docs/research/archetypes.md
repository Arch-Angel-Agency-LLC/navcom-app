# The Twelve

A stress-testing roster for field operations tooling. Deliberately weighted toward
operators who refuse, break or abandon software, because that's what surfaces
requirements early.

Structured on zodiac cusp pairings (element + modality) as the Archangel Agency class
convention. The astrology is scaffolding; the requirements are the point.

**Each archetype produces a requirement, not a veto.** See [README](./README.md).

> Constructed personas, not interviewed users.

---

### 1. Cusp of Rebirth — Pisces (Water Mutable) + Aries (Fire Cardinal)
**The Convert.** Formerly unhoused, now working the same blocks where she slept. Knows
which shelters actually take people who are using.

*"I want to add what I know without anyone asking who I am or how I know."*

→ **Requires:** standing that accrues through contribution alone, with no social history
and no permission. Anonymous contribution paths. A build that runs on a prepaid Android
8 with 400MB free.

### 2. Cusp of Power — Aries (Fire Cardinal) + Taurus (Earth Fixed)
**The Team Lead.** Runs a nine-person crew. Feels responsible if anyone gets hurt.

*"I want to see who's out and know they got home."*

→ **Requires:** team-scoped presence and op views that work without imposing a
hierarchy on operators who aren't in his crew. Oversight as an opt-in others grant, not
an authority he holds.

### 3. Cusp of Energy — Taurus (Earth Fixed) + Gemini (Air Mutable)
**The Quartermaster.** Owns the storage unit. Tracks socks, water, hygiene kits, sizes.

*"I want to know what we handed out so I know what to restock."*

→ **Requires:** supply signals that tolerate several people editing offline at once, and
resolve without a merge UI.

### 4. Cusp of Magic — Gemini (Air Mutable) + Cancer (Water Cardinal)
**The Connector.** Knows everyone in four cities. Makes introductions.

*"I want to tell Phoenix that Raven's coming through next month."*

→ **Requires:** cross-city presence and travel visibility. Also the reason op detail
must be scoped deliberately — he shares generously, because connecting people *is* his
contribution.

### 5. Cusp of Oscillation — Cancer (Water Cardinal) + Leo (Fire Fixed)
**The Heart.** Gives everything for six weeks, then vanishes for three months.

*"I want to come back after a break without a wall of guilt."*

→ **Requires:** missed check-in nudges the team, never declares an emergency. No
activity streaks, no absence commentary, no re-entry friction. Duress is always
deliberate, never inferred from silence.

### 6. Cusp of Exposure — Leo (Fire Fixed) + Virgo (Earth Mutable)
**The Public Face.** Large following, films everything, brings in donations.

*"I want to share what we did tonight."*

→ **Requires:** a shareable artifact that's safe by construction — his own record,
scrubbed of teammates' callsigns and positions. Give him something designed to leave
the app, or he'll screenshot something that shouldn't.

### 7. Cusp of Beauty — Virgo (Earth Mutable) + Libra (Air Cardinal)
**The Medic.** First aid and harm reduction. Precise, careful.

*"I want the naloxone supply list and the nearest ER that won't call police."*

→ **Requires:** volatile data always shows its age; stale reads "call first." Playbook
content good enough to stake an outcome on. Nothing in the app that could hold a
recipient's medical detail.

### 8. Cusp of Drama & Criticism — Libra (Air Cardinal) + Scorpio (Water Fixed)
**The Skeptic.** Twelve years in. Watched three apps come and go, and two people get
doxxed.

*"Show me exactly what leaves my phone or I'm not installing it."*

→ **Requires:** every network call explainable and auditable via proxy. No analytics, no
third-party push. Documented data tiers. Win him and adoption follows.

### 9. Cusp of Revolution — Scorpio (Water Fixed) + Sagittarius (Fire Mutable)
**The Protest Medic.** Street medic and legal observer at demonstrations.

*"I need this to be useless to anyone who takes my phone."*

→ **Requires:** panic wipe that truly destroys the wipeable tier while preserving
identity. No retained position history to subpoena. Discreet app name and icon.

### 10. Cusp of Prophecy — Sagittarius (Fire Mutable) + Capricorn (Earth Cardinal)
**The Trainer.** Teaches de-escalation and bystander intervention.

*"I want new people to arrive already knowing the ground rules."*

→ **Requires:** versioned playbooks with real authorship, and a contribution path good
enough that he'd put his own material in.

### 11. Cusp of Mystery & Imagination — Capricorn (Earth Cardinal) + Aquarius (Air Fixed)
**The Ghost.** Patrols alone. No team, no shared callsign, no position, ever.

*"I want the knowledge. I want to give nothing back."*

→ **Requires:** every social feature has an off switch, and the app is fully useful with
all of them off. Visibility defaults private. No prompt ever pressures him toward a team.

### 12. Cusp of Sensitivity — Aquarius (Air Fixed) + Pisces (Water Mutable)
**The Outpost.** Small town, two hours from the nearest peer, one bar of signal.

*"I want it to work in the parking lot with no service."*

→ **Requires:** full offline capability for the knowledge layer. Duress with SMS
fallback. Presence that shows the wider network, not an empty local map — for her,
seeing the country is the point.

## Coverage

| Risk surface | Archetype |
|---|---|
| Low-end hardware, limited data | Convert |
| Standing without social history | Convert |
| Team oversight vs. operator autonomy | Team Lead |
| Multi-writer offline sync | Quartermaster |
| Cross-city and travel | Connector, Outpost |
| Burnout, re-entry, alarm fatigue | Heart |
| Opsec violated from inside | Public Face |
| Safety-critical accuracy | Medic |
| Adversarial privacy audit | Skeptic |
| Device seizure, legal exposure | Protest Medic |
| Content quality, onboarding | Trainer |
| Total refusal of social features | Ghost |
| No connectivity, no local peers | Outpost |
